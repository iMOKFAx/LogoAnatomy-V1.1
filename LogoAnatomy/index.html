Guides

The guide is drawn on a locked layer called Logo Anatomy, on top of your artwork.

Improvements

Logo Anatomy points out what is almost aligned, almost mirrored or almost round, so you can decide. Sometimes breaking symmetry makes a logo better. There are no fixed rules in design: trust your eye, and fix only what you mean to.

LOGO ANATOMY v1.1
See how a logo is built, then make it cleaner.
HOW IT WORKS
  1. Select your logo and press Make Guide.
  2. See how it is built: circles, aligned points, angles, mirror axis and clear space.
  3. Improve what you want: Fix one spot, or Keep it if you meant it.
